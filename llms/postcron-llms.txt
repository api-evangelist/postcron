# Postcron

> Postcron is a social media scheduling and publishing tool for marketers, agencies and small
> businesses: plan, schedule and automatically publish content across Instagram, Facebook,
> X (Twitter), LinkedIn and Pinterest from one dashboard, with bulk upload from Excel/Google
> Docs, multi-account and team management, automatic image watermarking, a browser extension
> and iOS/Android apps. Postcron is an end-user product — as of 2026-08-13 it publishes no
> public developer API, no OpenAPI specification, no SDKs and no developer portal.

## Developer surface

- There is no Postcron developer program, API reference, OpenAPI/AsyncAPI specification,
  GraphQL endpoint, MCP server, A2A agent card, webhook catalog, CLI or client SDK.
- The hosts https://postcron.com/api/ and https://api.postcron.com/api/v1/ are the private
  backends of the Postcron web application. They answer, but they are token-gated
  (`401 You need send a token`) and undocumented; they are not offered to third-party
  developers and are not a supported integration surface.
- No `/.well-known/` discovery document is served on any Postcron host.
